Learn the art of Carnatic vocal music, from age 3 to adulthood

About Us

Carnatic vocal music, taught with tradition and care

KedaraIsaikoodam focuses exclusively on Carnatic vocal music — nurturing every student from their very first swara to the development of Manodharma, building a strong foundation in voice, raga, tala, repertoire, and the art of musical expression.

🎵

Age 3 to Adult

Structured beginner, intermediate and advanced levels for kids as young as 3, teenagers and adults.

📹

Online & Offline

Live online classes worldwide, home tutoring in Dubai, and a full centre in Chennai (Medavakkam) with both online and offline batches.

📚

Bridge Academy Syllabus

Our teachers are enrolled in the Bridge Academy syllabus, and students appear for Bridge Academy examinations.
